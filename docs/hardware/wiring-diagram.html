Nearling wiring

Every connection between the ESP32-S3 and the parts we have. Power and I2C go through four shared lines on the breadboard; every other signal is one wire of its own.

How it sits on the benchThe ESP32 stays off the breadboard. Each of its pins gets a female-to-male jumper: socket on the ESP32 pin, pin end in the breadboard.
The four shared lines3V3 is the red line, GND the blue line. SDA and SCL each get one empty breadboard column. Every part plugs into these instead of the ESP32 pin.
Go by the printed labelsThe pin order on this drawing is tidied up. On the real boards, find each pin by the name printed next to it.
Unplug USB firstAdd or move wires only with USB unplugged. A swapped VCC and GND is the one mistake that kills a part.
